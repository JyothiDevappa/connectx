FAQ

Before you apply

How long is the episode?

+

Most conversations run 40–50 minutes. We record more than we release, so the final cut stays tight and useful.

Do I need previous podcast experience?

+

No. Most of our guests have never recorded a podcast before. The screening call is there to make the actual recording feel like a normal conversation.

Is there a cost to be featured?

+

No. Being a guest is free. We don't accept paid placements or sponsored episodes — every guest is selected on the strength of their experience.

Can I choose the topic?

+

Yes. You tell us what you'd want to discuss in your application, and we shape the episode around it during the screening call.

When will my episode go live?

+

Typically 3–5 weeks after recording, once editing, clips and the spotlight page are ready.
