Questions, Answered

Frequently asked questions.

How do I become a YCX speaker?+

Fill out the application form above with your story and details. Our team reviews it and reaches out within 5–7 working days if it's a fit.

Who can apply?+

Students, entrepreneurs, founders, professionals, creators, mentors, educators, investors, and business leaders — if you have a story or expertise worth sharing, you're welcome to apply.

Is there a cost to speak at YCX?+

No. Speaking on YCX is free. We invest in producing your story because we believe it's worth telling well.

What languages can I speak in?+

Whatever language you're most fluent and expressive in. We currently support English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and are actively expanding to more.

Can I nominate someone else?+

Yes — use the "Someone else" option in the form and share as much as you can about their story. We'll take it from there.

What happens after I submit the form?+

Our team reviews every application. If shortlisted, we'll set up a short discovery call to learn more about your story and next steps.
